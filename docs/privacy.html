Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Web CO₂ Report Card is a browser extension that gives websites a carbon grade based on the data they load, and explains each grade. It runs entirely on your device.

What the extension reads

  • Network activity. The size of each network request a page makes, read from the browser's request events (webRequest) and the Performance API. The extension sees each request's address while measuring it but does not store or transmit it.
  • Sites you visit. The domain name of the site you're on (for example, example.com), used to grade the site and show your weekly summary.
  • Report details. When you open a "Why this grade?" report, the extension reads the list of files on that page (their addresses, sizes, types, and response headers such as compression) and the pixel dimensions of its images. This detail is kept in memory for the open tab only, cleared when the tab navigates or closes, and never stored or sent anywhere.

The extension does not store full URLs and does not read page text, form inputs, passwords, cookies, or personal information such as your name or email address.

What is stored, and for how long

Everything is stored in your browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your device:

  • Daily history: for each day, estimated grams of CO₂, the number of pages loaded, the domains you visited with their page counts, and estimated grams for your 25 heaviest sites that day. Kept for 365 days; older days are deleted automatically.
  • Site grades: for each site, the page weights of the last 20 pages you loaded there and the date you last visited. Up to 3,000 sites are kept; the least recently visited are removed first.
  • Totals: running CO₂ totals, a per-domain total, a page count, and the date tracking started.
  • Settings: your theme and whether tracking is on.

Byte counts for open tabs are held in temporary session storage and cleared when the browser closes.

How the data is used

This data is used only to show site grades, the "Why this grade?" report, and your weekly summary inside the extension. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

What is shared

Nothing. The extension makes no network requests and sends no data to the developer or any third party. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking. Data is never sold or transferred.

The use of information received by this extension adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Consent

Tracking is off when the extension is installed. Nothing is recorded until you read the disclosure on the welcome page or in the popup and choose Start tracking.

Deleting your data

Click Reset Counter in the extension popup to erase all stored history, totals, and site grades. Your theme setting is kept. Removing the extension deletes everything it stored.

Changes

If this policy or the extension's data practices change, the change will be described here and shown in the extension before it takes effect.
